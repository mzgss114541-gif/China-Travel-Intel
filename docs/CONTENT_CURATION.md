# Content Curation Standards & The Inbound Luxury Filter

This document specifies the editorial criteria used to filter raw official announcements and convert verified facts into actionable travel intelligence for affluent international travelers.

---

## Target Audience Profile

The content is tailored specifically for:
- **High-Net-Worth International Travelers**: Independent travelers (FITs), couples, families, and private group itineraries.
- **Languages**: English and Italian.
- **Travel Style**: Autonomous, discerning, interested in cultural heritage, bespoke overland expeditions, luxury scenic stays, and modern mobility (High-Speed Rail).
- **Core Pain Points**: Real-time museum ticket quotas, alpine weather holds, visa regulations, payment app setups, and train station navigation.

---

## The Inbound Luxury Filter

### 1. Inclusions (High-Impact Dispatches)

| Category | Typical Scenarios & Scope | Example Dispatches |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Heritage & Imperial Access** | UNESCO World Heritage sites, top national museums (Forbidden City, Mogao Caves, Terracotta Warriors, Sanxingdui). | Ticket quota notices, passport booking windows, hall restoration closures. |
| **Alpine & Scenic Advisories** | Severe weather disruptions in major scenic areas. | Changbaishan Heavenly Lake snow holds, Huangshan cable car wind stoppages, Jiuzhaigou capacity alerts. |
| **Cross-Regional Rail & Transit** | National High-Speed Rail trunk line schedules, premium trains. | Bullet train corridor additions, holiday relief trains, international airport transit shuttles. |
| **Immigration & Entry Policies** | Official national immigration & customs policies. | 15-day / 30-day visa exemption updates, 144-Hour TWOV transit, foreign credit card integration in Alipay/WeChat Pay. |
| **Seasonal Phenology** | Authentic natural & aesthetic markers. | First imperial snowfall, peak autumn foliage in national parks, prime blooming seasons. |

---

### 2. Exclusions (Strict Negative Blacklist)

| Rejected Category | Rationale for Exclusion |
|:---|:---|
| **Border Contraband & Seizures** | Smuggling of concealed mobile phones, undeclared cash, or cigarettes at land checkpoints has zero relevance to luxury tourists. |
| **Domestic Transit Sentimentalism** | Local human-interest anecdotes ("conductor assists lost passenger") provide no operational travel value. |
| **Local Community Sports** | County-level 5K runs, local marathons, suburban cycling meets. |
| **Resident-Only Concessions** | Government consumer coupons, local bus card discounts, student exam breaks. |
| **Internal Bureaucracy** | Departmental routine meetings, internal inspection tours, administrative cadres speeches. |
| **Everyday Moderate Weather** | Mild seasonal rain showers or light winds that do not disrupt transport or landmark operations. |
