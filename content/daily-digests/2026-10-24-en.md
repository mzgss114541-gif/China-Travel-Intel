# China Travel Daily Digest — October 24, 2026

**China Railway 2026 Train Numbering Plan, Nalati Panlong Valley Closure & Weekend Night HSR**
*Compiled and reviewed by YouTu Travel*
*Based on official announcements and transport authority notices.*

Good evening. Today is October 24, 2026. Welcome to the YouTu China Travel Daily Digest. Here are today's major field mobility and operational transit updates across the country.

China State Railway Group released details on its upcoming passenger train numbering reform starting in late November: 350 km/h Fuxing bullet trains will adopt new 'X' prefixes, while conventional services will transition T-trains to 'N' prefixes and K-trains to 'V' prefixes, providing clearer service class designations for travelers.
*Source: [China State Railway Group](https://weibo.com/u/2549511007) · Passenger train numbering reform framework announcement*

In Xinjiang, heavy snow and road icing led Nalati Scenic Area to enact the seasonal winter closure of its scenic Panlong Valley route starting October 25, directing tourists to snow-cleared valley routes.
*Sources: [Xinjiang Department of Culture & Tourism](https://weibo.com/u/2479930984) · Nalati Panlong Valley winter seasonal closure notice*

In the southwest, railway authorities fine-tuned schedules for the direct G8665/G8664 trains between Chengdu and Chongqing Jiangbei Airport, adding extra evening high-speed rail services to accommodate weekend travelers.
*Source: [China Railway Chengdu Group](https://weibo.com/u/1919955065) · Chengdu-Chongqing airport bullet train frequency adjustment notice*

That concludes today's China travel bulletin. See you tomorrow.
