# China Travel Daily Digest — November 1, 2026

**Enshi Pingshan Gorge Winter Closure, Ngari Sites Reopen & Dunhuang Expo Traffic Controls**
*Compiled and reviewed by YouTu Travel*
*Based on official announcements and transport authority notices.*

Good evening. Today is November 1, 2026. Welcome to the YouTu China Travel Daily Digest. Here are today's major field mobility and operational transit updates across the country.

In Hubei, Enshi's Pingshan Scenic Area enacted a temporary seasonal closure effective November 1 for winter infrastructure repairs and canyon safety enhancements, advising travelers to postpone gorge excursions.
*Sources: [Enshi Prefecture Bureau of Culture & Tourism](https://weibo.com/u/2061860827) · Winter canyon trail maintenance & seasonal closure announcement*

In western Tibet, four high-altitude cultural heritage destinations in Ngari—including the Guge Kingdom ruins, Piyang Donggar grottos, and Khyunglung Silver Castle—officially reopened following snow clearance. Foreign travelers must arrange visits through certified tour operators with regional permits.
*Sources: [Tibet Department of Culture & Tourism](https://weibo.com/u/2061847537) · Ngari high-altitude heritage reopenings & foreign traveler permit directives*

Across regional transport, Sichuan Airlines will resume direct Chengdu Tianfu–Jiuzhai Huanglong flights starting November 7 (operating Saturdays with a 55-minute flight time). Capital Airlines launched a seasonal Xi'an–Yongzhou–Xiamen route, and the new Chengdu Longquanyi Railway Station began commercial passenger operations.
*Sources: [Civil Aviation Administration of China, China Railway Chengdu Group](https://weibo.com/u/1919955065) · Winter air route activations & Chengdu Longquanyi rail station commissioning*

In Gansu, traffic authorities in [Dunhuang](/west) enacted strict vehicle diversion controls from November 2 to 4 for the Silk Road International Cultural Expo, barring transit trucks over 5 tons from urban roads and routing vehicles onto the G3011 and S31 expressways.
*Source: [Dunhuang Public Security Bureau](https://weibo.com/u/2128914281) · Silk Road Expo temporary traffic management & heavy vehicle bypass circular*

That concludes today's China travel bulletin. See you tomorrow.
