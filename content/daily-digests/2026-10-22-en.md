# China Travel Daily Digest — October 22, 2026

**Northern Gale and Temperature Drop Warnings, Xiling Snow Mountain Waiver & 144-Hour Transit**

Meteorological authorities and the Ministry of Emergency Management issued warnings for strong gales and temperature drops of 6 to 10°C across northern and western China, with snow and freezing rain affecting alpine passes in Xinjiang, Gansu, and Inner Mongolia. Drivers on mountain highways are advised to carry snow chains.

In seasonal promotions, Sichuan's Xiling Snow Mountain announced free main admission through November 30, while Mount Huangshan transitioned to off-peak opening hours. Shaanxi Hanzhong Airport added winter flights connecting Beijing, Shanghai, and Guangzhou.

At border ports including Guangzhou Baiyun, Beijing Daxing, and Shanghai Pudong, dedicated 144-hour visa-free transit inspection channels operated smoothly, further expediting customs clearance for international arrivals.
