# China Travel Daily Digest — November 7, 2026

**Ministry of Transport 12306 Ticketing Timeline, Multi-Province Blizzard Yellow Alert & Guangxi Themed Tourist Train**

The Ministry of Transport officially announced ticketing timelines for the upcoming passenger train renumbering plan taking effect at 00:00 on November 25. Tickets for newly designated passenger services—including standardized Z-prefix (express), N-prefix (fast), V-prefix (regular passenger), and Y-prefix (tourist) trains—will go on sale progressively through the official Railway 12306 platform starting November 11. Travelers planning rail itineraries in late November should confirm train numbering during booking.
*Source: [Ministry of Transport](https://weibo.com/u/7073634525) · Official passenger train numbering adjustment ticket release schedule*

Across [North China](/north), a strong cold front is driving severe snowfall, prompting the National Meteorological Center to issue a Yellow Warning for Blizzards. From 08:00 on November 7 to 08:00 on November 8, heavy snow to blizzard conditions are forecast across southeastern Inner Mongolia, western Liaoning, northern Shanxi, central-northern Hebei, western and northern Beijing, and northern Tianjin. Western Liaoning and northeastern Hebei face localized snowfalls of 20 to 29 mm with snow depths exceeding 15 cm. Within the Beijing metropolitan area, district-level Yellow Alerts for blizzards and road icing took effect across mountainous sectors including Yanqing, Huairou, Miyun, Pinggu, Changping, Mentougou, and Fangshan, creating slippery road conditions on passes leading to Badaling and Mutianyu Great Wall sections.
*Sources: [Ministry of Emergency Management, National Meteorological Center](https://weibo.com/u/5342220662), [Beijing Municipal Bureau of Culture and Tourism](https://weibo.com/u/1936009361) · Nationwide Yellow Warning for Blizzards & Beijing mountain icing safety alerts*

In [South China](/south), Guangxi launched its dedicated "Bagui Suofen" themed tourist train linking the prime scenic corridor of Nanning, Liuzhou, and Guilin. Train Y878 departs Nanning for Guilin North on November 7 and 15 (8:00 departure, 14:42 arrival), while train Y877 operates in the reverse direction on November 8 and 14 (8:30 departure, 15:10 arrival). Tickets are bookable directly on the official 12306 railway platform. Passengers holding valid train tickets or booking confirmations within two days can receive up to a 20% discount on rack rates at designated partner hotels and iconic attractions in Liuzhou and Guilin, including Two Rivers and Four Lakes, Reed Flute Cave, and Solitary Beauty Peak.
*Source: [Nanning Municipal Bureau of Culture and Tourism](https://weibo.com/u/1971263622) · Special tourist train schedule, amenities and ticketing guide*
