# China Travel Daily Digest — November 7, 2026

**Multi-Province Blizzard & Cold Wave Alerts, Chengdu–Jiuzhaigou Winter Flight Resumption & Guangxi Themed Tourist Train**

Across [North China](/north), an intensifying winter cold front is driving severe blizzards and road icing, prompting the National Meteorological Center to issue concurrent Yellow Alerts for Blizzards alongside Blue Alerts for Cold Waves and Gales. From 20:00 on November 7 to 20:00 on November 8, heavy snow to localized extreme blizzards (30 to 32 mm) are forecast across western Liaoning, northeastern Hebei, Beijing, northern Tianjin, and southeastern Inner Mongolia, with snow depths reaching 5 to 15 cm and exceeding 20 cm locally. Within the Beijing metropolitan area, district-level Yellow Alerts for blizzards and road icing took effect across mountainous sectors including Yanqing, Huairou, Miyun, Pinggu, Changping, Mentougou, and Fangshan, creating slippery road conditions on passes leading to Badaling and Mutianyu Great Wall sections. In Hebei, severe snow and trail icing prompted Wuyuezhai Scenic Area in Shijiazhuang to advance its annual winter closure from November 9 to November 8, with full ticket refunds provided. Furthermore, the sweeping cold front will plunge temperatures nationwide by 6 to 10°C (locally over 14°C) through November 10, accompanied by inland scale 5 to 6 winds and scale 7 to 8 coastal gales.
*Sources: [National Meteorological Center](https://weibo.com/u/2015108055), [Ministry of Emergency Management](https://weibo.com/u/5342220662), [Beijing Municipal Bureau of Culture and Tourism](https://weibo.com/u/1936009361), [Shijiazhuang Municipal Bureau of Culture and Tourism](https://weibo.com/u/2516150701) · Nationwide blizzard and cold wave warnings, Beijing icing alerts & Wuyuezhai winter closure*

Across [West China](/west), Sichuan Airlines resumed direct scheduled flights between Chengdu Tianfu International Airport and Jiuzhai Huanglong Airport on November 7, restoring fast air access to the alpine national park for the winter season. Operating once weekly on Saturdays, outbound flight 3U6675 departs Chengdu at 12:50 and lands at Jiuzhai Huanglong at 13:45, with return flight 3U6676 departing at 14:25 and arriving in Chengdu at 15:20. The 55-minute flight provides a winter alternative to overland rail and road routes, with one-way economy tickets priced from 300 RMB before tax.
*Source: [Aba Prefecture Bureau of Culture and Tourism](https://weibo.com/u/6608349086) · Chengdu-Jiuzhai Huanglong winter flight resumption schedule & fares*

In [South China](/south), Guangxi launched its dedicated "Bagui Suofen" themed tourist train linking the prime scenic corridor of Nanning, Liuzhou, and Guilin. Train Y878 departs Nanning for Guilin North on November 7 and 15 (8:00 departure, 14:42 arrival), while train Y877 operates in the reverse direction on November 8 and 14 (8:30 departure, 15:10 arrival), both bookable directly on 12306. Passengers holding valid train tickets or booking confirmations within two days can receive up to a 20% discount on rack rates at designated partner hotels and iconic attractions in Liuzhou and Guilin, including Two Rivers and Four Lakes, Reed Flute Cave, and Solitary Beauty Peak.
*Source: [Nanning Municipal Bureau of Culture and Tourism](https://weibo.com/u/1971263622) · Special tourist train schedule, amenities and ticketing guide*
