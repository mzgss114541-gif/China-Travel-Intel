# China Travel Daily Digest — November 7, 2026

**Ministry of Transport 12306 Ticketing Timeline, Multi-Province Blizzard Yellow Alert & Guangxi Themed Tourist Train**

In **National Transit**, the Ministry of Transport officially announced ticketing timelines for the upcoming passenger train renumbering plan taking effect at 00:00 on November 25. Tickets for newly designated passenger services—including standardized Z-prefix (express), N-prefix (fast), V-prefix (regular passenger), and Y-prefix (tourist) trains—will go on sale progressively through the official Railway 12306 platform starting November 11. Travelers planning rail itineraries in late November should confirm train numbering during booking.
*Source: [Ministry of Transport](https://weibo.com/u/7073634525) · Official passenger train numbering adjustment ticket release schedule*

Across [North China](/north), winter weather disruptions escalated as the National Meteorological Center issued a Yellow Warning for Blizzards. From 08:00 on November 7 to 08:00 on November 8, severe snowfall is forecast across southeastern Inner Mongolia, western Liaoning, northern Shanxi, central-northern Hebei, western and northern Beijing, and northern Tianjin. Western Liaoning and northeastern Hebei face heavy blizzard conditions with 20 to 29 mm of snowfall and fresh snow accumulation depths between 5 and 15 cm, exceeding 20 cm locally. Travelers should prepare for potential delays across highway networks and regional rail lines.
*Source: [Ministry of Emergency Management, National Meteorological Center](https://weibo.com/u/5342220662) · Nationwide Yellow Warning for Blizzards and snow depth forecasts*

Within the Beijing metropolitan area, tourism and emergency authorities released district-level Yellow Alerts for blizzards and road icing across [North China](/north). From noon on November 7 through the morning of November 8, outer and mountainous districts—including Yanqing, Huairou, Miyun, Pinggu, Changping, Mentougou, and Fangshan—face heavy snow to blizzard conditions, with 12-hour snowfall reaching 6 mm or more. Mountain passes leading to sections of the Great Wall at Badaling and Mutianyu, as well as suburban heritage sites, face icy road conditions and freezing temperatures.
*Source: [Beijing Municipal Bureau of Culture and Tourism](https://weibo.com/u/1936009361) · Multi-district blizzard and road icing safety alerts*

In [South China](/south), Guangxi launched its dedicated "Bagui Suofen" themed tourist train linking the prime scenic corridor of Nanning, Liuzhou, and Guilin. Train Y878 departs Nanning for Guilin North on November 7 and 15 (8:00 departure, 14:42 arrival), while train Y877 operates in the reverse direction on November 8 and 14 (8:30 departure, 15:10 arrival). Tickets are bookable directly on the official 12306 railway platform. Passengers holding valid train tickets or booking confirmations within two days can receive up to a 20% discount on rack rates at designated partner hotels and iconic attractions in Liuzhou and Guilin, including Two Rivers and Four Lakes, Reed Flute Cave, and Solitary Beauty Peak.
*Source: [Nanning Municipal Bureau of Culture and Tourism](https://weibo.com/u/1971263622) · Special tourist train schedule, amenities and ticketing guide*
