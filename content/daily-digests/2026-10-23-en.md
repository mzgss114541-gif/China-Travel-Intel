# China Travel Daily Digest — October 23, 2026

**Qinghai-Tibet Railway Controlled Resumption Post-Quake & Chang'an Airlines Winter Network**
*Compiled and reviewed by YouTu Travel*
*Based on official announcements and transport authority notices.*

Good evening. Today is October 23, 2026. Welcome to the YouTu China Travel Daily Digest. Here are today's major field mobility and operational transit updates across the country.

Following an earthquake in Baqen County, Tibet, passenger trains on the Qinghai-Tibet Railway were temporarily held for emergency track inspections. Once bridge integrity and track geometry were confirmed, held services resumed under guided speed restrictions to safeguard travelers.
*Source: [China Railway Qinghai-Tibet Group](https://weibo.com/u/2061858797) · Post-earthquake railway emergency response & controlled restart bulletin*

In aviation and rail tourism, Chang'an Airlines announced its winter schedule operating from Xi'an across 41 destinations. Simultaneously, China Railway Chengdu Group launched the 'Colorful Guizhou' sleeper tourist train connecting Guiyang, Zunyi, Chongqing, Chengdu, and Panzhihua for cross-provincial leisure travel.
*Sources: [Chang'an Airlines, China Railway Chengdu Group](https://weibo.com/u/1919955065) · Winter aviation flight network & tourist sleeper train schedule*
