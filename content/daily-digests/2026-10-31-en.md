**Customs Foreign Currency Cash Limits, Guangxi Rail Passes & G219 Snow Controls**

Good evening. Today is October 31, 2026. Welcome to the YouTu China Travel Daily Digest. Here are today's major field mobility and operational transit updates across the country.

Customs officers at West Kowloon High-Speed Rail Station intercepted $20,000 USD in undeclared cash from an exiting traveler. Under official customs regulations, travelers departing China for the second time or more within 15 days are legally permitted to carry foreign currency cash equivalent to no more than $1,000 USD without an authorized bank export permit; unpermitted excess cash will not be cleared.
*Source: [General Administration of Customs of China](https://weibo.com/u/5832321505) · Statutory foreign cash export limit regulations for frequent border travelers*

In the south, China Railway Nanning Group expanded its 'Roam Bagui' high-speed rail multi-ride passes from 6 to 50 stations across [Guangxi](/south). Valid for 15 days, travelers can combine 2 to 10 journey segments across Guilin, Liuzhou, Nanning, and Beihai with a 10% discount via 12306.
*Source: [China Railway Nanning Group](https://weibo.com/u/1971263622) · Multi-trip regional high-speed rail pass network expansion notice*

Weather alerts in Guizhou warned that heavy localized rain on November 1–2 could trigger precautionary speed restrictions on the Guiyang–Nanning and Guiyang–Guangzhou HSR lines. In Tibet, Saga County traffic police implemented two-way controls along Highway G219 due to severe snow and icing.
*Sources: [Ministry of Emergency Management, Tibet Traffic Police](https://weibo.com/u/5342220662) · Severe weather alerts for mountain high-speed rail & G219 alpine pass icing*
