# China Travel Daily Digest — November 4, 2026

**Dali Cableway Maintenance, Wuxi 50% Scenic Spot Admission & Guangzhou Light Festival**
*Compiled and reviewed by YouTu Travel*
*Based on official announcements and transport authority notices.*

Good evening. Today is November 4, 2026. Welcome to the YouTu China Travel Daily Digest. Here are today's major field mobility and operational transit updates across the country.

In Yunnan, the Dali Culture and Tourism Bureau announced that the [Cangshan Gantong Cableway](/west) suspends operations on November 5 for scheduled equipment safety testing. Ximatan and Zhonghe ropeways remain open as alternative routes to the mountain summit, with normal operations resuming on November 6.
*Sources: [Dali Culture & Tourism Bureau](https://weibo.com/u/1959521522) · Cangshan cableway annual safety maintenance notice*

Across Jiangsu, tourism authorities in [Wuxi](/east) have introduced a 50% admission discount across all major state-managed Grade-A attractions—including Lingshan Grand Buddha and Yuantouzhu—for domestic and international visitors through November 22. Online reservation is required. Note that due to the China Road Cycling Race on November 7, Mashan sector roads will close to vehicle traffic, pausing promotional admission at Lingshan for that day.
*Sources: [Wuxi Municipal Bureau of Culture & Tourism](https://weibo.com/u/2123512965) · Autumn scenic spot half-price ticketing campaign & cycling race road closure advisory*

Across [North and Northeast China](/north), a severe cold front is driving sharp temperature drops exceeding 10°C alongside gale-force gusts and freezing rain across Heilongjiang and Inner Mongolia. Harbin Railway mobilized switch heating crews along alpine corridors, while 10- and 20-ride multi-trip rail passes for the Beijing-Baotou HSR covering Badaling Great Wall took effect across 15 stations.
*Sources: [China Railway Harbin Group, Beijing Railway](https://weibo.com/u/1957405325) · Alpine rail switch de-icing operations & Beijing-Baotou multi-trip pass activation*

At national entry points, Shanghai Pudong International Airport border inspection activated dedicated clearance channels ahead of the 9th China International Import Expo (CIIE). In Liaoning, Dalian Airport border crossings surpassed 1 million this year, with visa-free foreign arrivals rising by over 18%.
*Source: [National Immigration Administration](https://weibo.com/u/6929716472) · Dedicated CIIE port clearance channels & annual international border crossing statistics*

In the [Greater Bay Area](/south), Guangzhou officially announced dates for its 15th annual international light festival, running from December 4 to 14 across 36 art installations, featuring a coordinated light show between Canton Tower, East Tower, and West Tower. Haixinsha core venue requires free advance registration starting December 1 at 12:00 CST, while waterfront public promenades remain unticketed.
*Sources: [Guangzhou Municipal Bureau of Culture & Tourism](https://weibo.com/u/2532579261) · 15th Guangzhou International Light Festival official schedule & Haixinsha registration rules*

That concludes today's China travel bulletin. See you tomorrow.
