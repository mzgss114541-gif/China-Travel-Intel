### Regional Dispatches

#### 📍 [【West China Corridor】](/west)
Dali Tourism Bureau announced that the Cangshan Gantong Cableway will suspend operations on November 5 for scheduled equipment safety testing. Ximatan and Zhonghe cableways remain open as alternative routes to the mountain summit, with normal operations resuming on November 6.  
*(Source: [Dali Culture and Tourism Bureau (Sina Weibo)](https://weibo.com/u/1959521522) · [Explore West China Hub &rarr;](/west) &bull; Monitored & Verified by YouTu Travel)*

The "Colorful Guizhou" sleeper tourist express resumed operations between Guiyang and Panzhihua South, stopping at Zunyi, Chongqing West, Chengdu West, Emei, and Xichang West. Additionally, Nalati Scenic Area in Xinjiang temporarily suspended online self-driving tour reservations for on-site processing at the visitor service center; Wawushan and Hailuogou in Sichuan rolled out seasonal admission discounts.  
*(Source: [Guiyang Culture & Tourism](https://weibo.com/u/1919955065) · [Xinjiang Department of Culture and Tourism](https://weibo.com/u/2479930984) &bull; Monitored & Verified by YouTu Travel)*

#### 📍 [【East China & Jiangnan】](/east)
Wuxi tourism authorities announced a 50% first-gate ticket discount across major state-managed Grade-A attractions—including Lingshan Grand Buddha and Yuantouzhu—for domestic and international visitors through November 22. Online reservation is required. Note: due to the road cycling race on November 7, Mashan sector roads will close and Lingshan promotions will pause for that day.  
*(Source: [Wuxi Municipal Bureau of Culture and Tourism (Sina Weibo)](https://weibo.com/u/2123512965) · [Explore East China Hub &rarr;](/east) &bull; Monitored & Verified by YouTu Travel)*

Zhejiang extended its free Quzhou promotional access through late March 2027; Liangzhu Ancient City Archaeological Site Park entered peak golden rice paddy viewing season; Haihunhou National Heritage Park in Jiangxi launched a free admission challenge for reciting the Analects.  
*(Source: [Zhejiang Department of Culture and Tourism](https://weibo.com/u/2069794017) &bull; Monitored & Verified by YouTu Travel)*

#### 📍 [【North China & Great Wall】](/north)
A strong cold front is driving temperature drops exceeding 10°C with gale gusts and freezing rain across Heilongjiang and Inner Mongolia. Harbin Railway mobilized 300 crew members to maintain switch heaters in the Daxing'anling alpine line. In Beijing, multi-trip passes for the Beijing-Baotou HSR covering Badaling Great Wall were officially enabled across 15 stations.  
*(Source: [Ministry of Emergency Management](https://weibo.com/u/5342220662) · [Harbin Railway](https://weibo.com/u/1957405325) · [Grassland Railway](https://weibo.com/u/1920077735) · [Explore North China Hub &rarr;](/north) &bull; Monitored & Verified by YouTu Travel)*

#### 📍 [【National Policy & Inbound Ports】](/transit)
Ahead of the 9th China International Import Expo opening in Shanghai, Pudong International Airport border inspection activated dedicated clearance channels for international attendees. Dalian Airport border crossings surpassed 1 million this year, with visa-free inbound foreign visitors rising by over 18%. In addition, China Railway launched its 20-day high-speed rail express parcel service across 196 major transport hub cities.  
*(Source: [National Immigration Administration (Sina Weibo)](https://weibo.com/u/6929716472) · [Ministry of Transport](https://weibo.com/u/7073634525) · [National Policy Hub &rarr;](/transit) &bull; Monitored & Verified by YouTu Travel)*

#### 📍 [【South China & Greater Bay】](/south)
Guangzhou officially announced its 15th annual light festival across 36 art installations, featuring a coordinated light show between Canton Tower, East Tower, and West Tower. Haixinsha core venue requires free advance registration starting December 1 at 12:00 CST; waterfront public promenades remain unticketed. In Kaifeng, arriving rail passengers can exchange same-day train tickets for cultural activity vouchers.
