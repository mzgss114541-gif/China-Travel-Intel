# China Travel Daily Digest — November 6, 2026

**National Railway Numbering Implementation, Harbin-Yabuli Airport Express & Hebei Orange Blizzard Warning**
*Compiled and reviewed by YouTu Travel*
*Based on official announcements and transport authority notices.*

Good evening. Today is November 6, 2026. Welcome to the YouTu China Travel Daily Digest. Here are today's major field mobility and operational transit updates across the country.

In [National Transit](/transit), China State Railway Group released execution arrangements for the 2026 passenger train numbering reform. Taking effect at 00:00 on November 25, conventional passenger trains will adopt standardized letter designations: "Z" remains for direct express services, "N" replaces "T" for express services, "V" replaces "K" for fast passenger trains, and "Y" is dedicated exclusively to tourist trains. Ticket sales for newly designated services commence November 11, with Beijing-Shanghai pilot reservations being fulfilled starting November 6; high-speed train numbers remain unchanged until late December.
*Source: [China State Railway Group](http://www.china-railway.com.cn/xwzx/ywsl/202611/t20261106_161244.html) · 2026 train numbering reform rollout schedule*

Across [North China](/north), transport connectivity expanded as a direct airport express coach opened between Harbin Taiping International Airport and the Yabuli Ski Resort, covering 260 km in approximately three hours with four daily departures in each direction. Meanwhile, Heilongjiang culture and tourism authorities reported that over 7,200 foreign card-enabled ATMs and POS terminals have been installed across airports, border ports, and major scenic areas to facilitate cashless and international card payments. In Hebei, meteorological authorities issued an Orange Warning for Blizzards for November 7–8, warning that northern mountain sectors including Zhangjiakou, Chengde, and northern Tangshan may experience heavy snowfalls between 20 and 30 mm.
*Sources: [Heilongjiang Culture & Tourism Department, Hebei Meteorological Observatory](https://weibo.com/u/5607433524) · Harbin-Yabuli shuttle launch, inbound payment accessibility & regional blizzard warnings*

In [South China](/south) and cross-border corridors, railway authorities highlighted direct high-speed services from Guangxi to Hong Kong West Kowloon, linking Nanning East in 3 hours 57 minutes and Guilin West in 3 hours 11 minutes under the co-location clearance model. In Hunan, Yongzhou Railway Station completed an operational switch on November 6, putting newly constructed platforms 4 to 7 into passenger service while retiring old platforms 1 to 3 as construction progresses on the 350 km/h Shaoyang-Yongzhou high-speed line.
*Sources: [China Railway Nanning Group, Hunan Culture & Tourism](https://weibo.com/u/1869124960) · Guangxi-Hong Kong direct HSR connections & Yongzhou platform switchover*

Across [West China](/west), the 9th China Intangible Cultural Heritage Expo opened at the Chongqing International Convention and Exhibition Centre in Nan'an district, running through November 10 with free advance real-name registration via official mini-program and direct transit at Metro Line 3 Gongmao Station. In Shaanxi, four natural scenic areas—Huangling National Forest Park, Guanshan Grassland, Heihe National Forest Park, and Yehe Mountain—announced seasonal winter closures or roadwork halts with full ticket refunds via original purchase channels, while alpine destinations across Qinghai entered winter dormancy.
*Sources: [Chongqing Jiulongpo Culture & Tourism, Xi'an Tourism Bureau, Qinghai Tourism](https://weibo.com/u/3077531287) · 9th Intangible Cultural Heritage Expo & western winter scenic closures*

In [East China](/east), traffic authorities in Zhoushan announced that the Zhujiajian Strait Bridge will undergo lane-occupying structural safety inspections on weekdays between November 11 and 18, advising travelers heading to Zhoushan Putuoshan Airport and ferry piers to budget additional road transit time.
*Source: [Zhoushan Culture & Tourism Bureau](https://weibo.com/u/1991383002) · Zhujiajian Strait Bridge maintenance and lane management advisory*

That concludes today's China travel bulletin. See you tomorrow.
