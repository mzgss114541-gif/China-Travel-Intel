# China Travel Daily Digest — November 3, 2026

**Fuzhou Air-Rail Airport Express Shuttles & Chengdu International Route Resumptions**
*Compiled and reviewed by YouTu Travel*
*Based on official announcements and transport authority notices.*

Good evening. Today is November 3, 2026. Welcome to the YouTu China Travel Daily Digest. Here are today's major field mobility and operational transit updates across the country.

In Fujian, transport authorities launched a free airport express shuttle between Fuzhou South Railway Station and Changle International Airport through December 31. High-speed rail passengers holding connecting flight itineraries can redeem a free transfer ticket upon presenting their ID card and travel booking, providing seamless baggage transit between rail and air.
*Sources: [Fuzhou Municipal Bureau of Culture & Tourism](https://weibo.com/u/2061860827) · Free air-rail multimodal airport transfer policy*

Following the civil aviation winter-spring season change, authorities in Chengdu outlined upcoming international passenger flight resumptions. Sichuan Airlines will resume non-stop flights between Chengdu and Auckland on December 3, followed by Dubai on January 1, 2027. Air China will launch non-stop Chengdu Tianfu–Hanoi flights in January, while regular service to Abu Dhabi begins in early March, strengthening long-haul connections into [West China](/west).
*Sources: [Chengdu Municipal Bureau of Culture & Tourism, Sichuan Airlines](https://weibo.com/u/2164623652) · Winter international route resumption schedule & transcontinental flight expansion*
