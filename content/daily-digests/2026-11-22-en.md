# China Travel Daily Digest — November 22, 2026

**Weekly Beijing–Ulaanbaatar–Irkutsk V3/4 International Train Starts Nov 25, Qinghai–Tibet Railway Xining–Golmud Section Reopens, Yulong Snow Mountain Snow & Cableway Notice, and Scenic Closures in Yichang and Changchun**

Across [North China](/north), the weekly V3/4 China–Mongolia–Russia international passenger train begins round-trip operations between Beijing, Ulaanbaatar, and Irkutsk on November 25 (2,666 km, 55 hours total), calling at Beijing, Jiningnan, Erenhot, Zamyn-Uud, Sainshand, Choir, Ulaanbaatar, Züünkharaa, Darkhan, Sukhbaatar, Naushki, Ulan-Ude, and Irkutsk, with border formalities at Erenhot, Zamyn-Uud, Sukhbaatar, and Naushki. Train V3 departs Beijing Station Wednesdays at 07:27 (arriving Ulaanbaatar Thursday at 14:35 and Irkutsk Friday at 14:55); train 4 departs Irkutsk Saturdays at 06:58 (arriving Ulaanbaatar Sunday at 06:38 and Beijing Monday at 14:35). Tickets are sold offline at international rail agencies in Beijing (Room 819, Guolv Building, No. 1 Dongdan North Street; tel: 010-65120507) and Erenhot (No. 0001 Yingbin South Road; tel: 0479-7521096). Separately, Changchun World Sculpture Park announced a temporary closure.
*Sources: [China Railway Nanchang Group](https://weibo.com/u/1882716230), [Changchun Culture and Tourism](https://weibo.com/u/2798034700)*

Across [West China](/west), at 23:10 on November 21, after inspections confirmed track and equipment integrity following the earthquake in Delingha, Qinghai, the block on the Xining–Golmud section of the Qinghai–Tibet Railway was lifted and held passenger trains gradually resumed running under speed restrictions; travelers should check station announcements or Railway 12306 for updated schedules. In Yunnan, Yulong Snow Mountain Scenic Area canceled visitor reception for select time slots on the Glacier Park Cableway, while its weather forecast indicates sunny to partly cloudy conditions on November 23 followed by showers or sleet on November 24–25 with moderate to heavy snow at the Glacier Park upper station.
*Sources: [China Railway Qinghai-Tibet Group](https://weibo.com/u/2286736447), [Yulong Snow Mountain Scenic Area](https://weibo.com/u/5288549152)*

Across [South China](/south), Chaibuxi Grand Canyon Scenic Area in Yichang, Hubei announced a full closure for upgrading and construction, with ticket refunds being processed. Separately, after Guangzhou Baiyun Airport Customs intercepted an inbound traveler in the Green Channel carrying EUR 260,600 in undeclared cash, Customs reiterated that inbound passengers carrying over RMB 20,000 in RMB cash or foreign currency cash equivalent to over USD 5,000 must declare truthfully and use the Red Channel.
*Sources: [Yichang Culture and Tourism](https://weibo.com/u/2140270927), [China Customs](https://weibo.com/u/5832321505)*
