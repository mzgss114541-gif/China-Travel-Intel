# China Travel Daily Digest — October 27, 2026

**Railway 12306 Group Ticket Directory, Nanning ASEAN Flight Expansion & Mohan Port Advisory**
*Compiled and reviewed by YouTu Travel*
*Based on official announcements and transport authority notices.*

Good evening. Today is October 27, 2026. Welcome to the YouTu China Travel Daily Digest. Here are today's major field mobility and operational transit updates across the country.

China Railway's official 12306 platform launched a dedicated station contact directory query service, allowing passenger groups of 20 or more traveling on the same itinerary to coordinate directly with station ticketing personnel at least 3 days in advance without queuing at physical ticket windows.
*Source: [China State Railway Group (Railway 12306)](https://weibo.com/u/2549511007) · Nationwide group ticket contact directory service launch notice*

With the winter-spring flight schedule active, Nanning Airport added 15 non-stop international passenger routes to ASEAN capitals including Bangkok, Singapore, and Manila, expanding air-rail baggage check-in facilities. Civil aviation in Tibet increased Lhasa–Kathmandu scheduled direct flights to a daily Monday-to-Friday frequency.
*Source: [Civil Aviation Administration of China](https://weibo.com/u/1971263622) · Winter schedule international flight additions across Guangxi & Tibet corridors*

On the China-Laos Railway, Kunming Railway reminded travelers that Mohan Railway Port does not issue visas on arrival, requiring international travelers to hold pre-arranged visas before departure. Meanwhile, Zhangjiakou Ningyuan Airport launched free express shuttle buses connecting arriving passengers directly to Chongli ski resorts.
*Sources: [China Railway Kunming Group, Hebei Tourism](https://weibo.com/u/1920078035) · Mohan international rail border visa reminder & Chongli ski shuttle service*

That concludes today's China travel bulletin. See you tomorrow.
