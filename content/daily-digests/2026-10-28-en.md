**Everest Temporary Blizzard Closure, East China Free Scenic Passes & Shaanxi HSR Tickets**

Good evening. Today is October 28, 2026. Welcome to the YouTu China Travel Daily Digest. Here are today's major field mobility and operational transit updates across the country.

Severe blizzard warnings prompted traffic police in Shigatse, Tibet, to enforce emergency highway controls across Yadong, Nyalam, Gyirong, and Tingri counties starting October 29. The Mount Everest Scenic Area closed temporarily to safeguard visitors while road crews monitored pass conditions.
*Source: [Shigatse Public Security Bureau](https://weibo.com/u/2257962015) · Precautionary Mount Everest scenic area closure & border snow advisory*

In Zhejiang, Qiandao Lake announced free admission across its Central and Southeast lake areas for five months from November 1 to March 31, alongside 30% discounts on boat cruises. Quzhou made 14 core A-level scenic areas free to all global visitors starting November 1 via online booking, and Xinchang County waived admission to Dafo Temple and Shijiu Peaks through November 30.
*Sources: [Zhejiang Provincial Department of Culture & Tourism](https://weibo.com/u/2069794017) · Qiandao Lake & Quzhou winter free admission initiatives announcement*

China Railway Xi'an Group launched 20-ride multi-trip rail passes on routes between Yan'an, Manchuanguan, and Xi'an North with discounts up to 20%. At Wenjindu Port in Shenzhen, customs officers intercepted an outbound traveler carrying 213,000 HKD in excess undeclared cash, reminding passengers of the $1,000 USD limit for frequent travelers.
*Sources: [China Railway Xi'an Group, General Administration of Customs](https://weibo.com/u/2197723802) · Shaanxi multi-trip high-speed rail passes & outbound currency inspection report*
