# China Travel Daily Digest — November 5, 2026

**China Railway 2026 Numbering Reform, Western Scenic Maintenance & Nanning-Vietnam Route**
*Compiled and reviewed by YouTu Travel*
*Based on official announcements and transport authority notices.*

Good evening. Today is November 5, 2026. Welcome to the YouTu China Travel Daily Digest. Here are today's major field mobility and operational transit updates across the country.

China State Railway Group announced implementation details for the first phase of its nationwide passenger train numbering reform. Starting at 0:00 on November 25, conventional passenger trains will transition to standardized letter prefixes: T-services will transition to 'N' prefixes (fast trains up to 140 km/h), while K-services will transition to 'V' prefixes (regular passenger trains up to 120 km/h). The 'Z' prefix (express trains up to 160 km/h) remains unchanged, and 'Y' prefixes are reserved exclusively for tourist trains. Ticket sales for newly designated services begin November 11, with Beijing-Shanghai Railway pilot reservations honoring from November 6; high-speed EMU numbering will remain unchanged until late December.
*Source: China State Railway Group · Official passenger train numbering reform announcement*

Across [West China](/west), Kunming Jiaozishan Mountain Cableway in Yunnan has suspended operations for scheduled equipment maintenance through November 12, resuming November 13. In Shaanxi, Heihe National Forest Park announced temporary closures starting immediately for highway repairs, with full ticket refunds provided. In Sichuan, Ganzi's Wuxuhai Scenic Area introduced free admission through November 8, while Gongga Mountain Nature Reserve authorities issued strict safety warnings reminding hikers that unauthorized alpine trekking routes remain strictly prohibited.
*Sources: Kunming Bureau of Culture & Tourism, Shaanxi Dept of Culture & Tourism · Equipment maintenance & park transit notices*

In [South China](/south) and cross-border transport, Beibu Gulf Airlines will inaugurate non-stop scheduled passenger flights connecting Nanning and Haiphong, Vietnam on November 10, operating Tuesdays and Saturdays with a one-hour flight time. In Guangxi, railway authorities will run a dedicated regional culinary tourism train on November 7 connecting Nanning, Liuzhou, and Guilin.
*Source: Nanning Tourism Bureau · Aviation & culinary rail service notices*

Across [East China](/east), China Railway Nanchang Group scheduled extra passenger trains linking Jingdezhen, Wuyuan, and Changsha for autumn travel. In addition, railway operators partnered with regional scenic spots in Jiangxi and Fujian to roll out ticket stub discounts, granting arriving rail passengers 50% discounts on admission tickets across Mount Wugong partner areas and Yuhua Cave.
*Source: China Railway Nanchang Group · Autumn timetable & scenic partnership notice*
