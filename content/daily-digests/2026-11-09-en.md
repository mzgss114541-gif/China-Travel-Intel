# China Travel Daily Digest — November 9, 2026

**Tibet Blizzard Closures on Namtso & G216 Highway, Zhoushan Putuoshan Cableway & Ferry Suspensions, and Cross-Provincial Rail Capacity Additions**

Across [West China](/west), sudden heavy snowfall and severe gales created hazardous road icing, prompting emergency closures and traffic restrictions across alpine corridors in Tibet. Namtso Shengxiang Tianmen Scenic Area suspended tourist access on November 9 as maintenance crews conduct snow removal operations, with reopening dates to be announced following safety assessments; full refunds are available for unused tickets, while Namtso Zhaxi Island Scenic Area remains open as normal. Along the primary overland corridor toward the Gyirong border port, blizzard weather prompted transport authorities to extend road maintenance traffic controls on National Highway G216 between the Kongtang LASHAN Tunnel entrance and Gyirong County seat (km 3415+561 to km 3428+876, 13.315 km) through 24:00 on November 15. The highway section is completely closed daily during three periods: 8:30–12:30, 13:30–17:00, and 18:00–23:00; transit is permitted from 23:00 to 8:30 the following morning, as well as 12:30–13:30 and 17:00–18:00, with a strict 10 km/h speed limit throughout the construction zone.
*Sources: [Tibet Autonomous Region Department of Culture and Tourism](https://weibo.com/u/2061860827), [Shigatse Municipal Bureau of Culture and Tourism](https://weibo.com/u/2257962015) · Namtso Shengxiang Tianmen closure advisory & G216 Gyirong highway traffic control extension*

Across [East China](/east), gale-force winds driven by an advancing cold air mass disrupted island and mountain transit links across the Zhoushan archipelago in Zhejiang. Mount Putuo (Putuoshan) Ropeway suspended operations on November 9, with resumption pending updated meteorological assessments. Maritime passenger ferry services between Putuoshan and Luojia Mountain were suspended starting November 9, while primary passenger ferries between Zhujiajian and Putuoshan halted operations on November 9 with services expected to resume on November 10 subject to marine weather. Travelers connecting through Zhoushan ports should check real-time terminal schedules before departing.
*Source: [Zhoushan Municipal Bureau of Culture, Radio, Television, Tourism and Sports](https://weibo.com/u/1991383002) · Putuoshan cableway suspension & maritime passenger ferry halts*

Connecting [South China](/south) and [East China](/east), China Railway Guangzhou Group scheduled 22 additional round-trip temporary passenger train services between Shenzhen East and Nanchang via Dongguan East from November 12 to 23 to alleviate regional passenger demand. Overnight train K4362 operates daily from November 12 to 22, departing Shenzhen East at 18:20, stopping at Dongguan East from 19:13 to 19:19, and arriving in Nanchang at 7:51 the following morning. Return service K4361 runs daily from November 13 to 23, departing Nanchang at 8:51, calling at Dongguan East from 18:50 to 18:56, and terminating at Shenzhen East at 19:34. Tickets are available for booking via Railway 12306.
*Source: [China Railway Guangzhou Group](https://weibo.com/u/1923237421) · Shenzhen East–Nanchang temporary passenger train schedules & ticketing*
