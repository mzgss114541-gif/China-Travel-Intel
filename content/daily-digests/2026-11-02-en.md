# China Travel Daily Digest — November 2, 2026

**Extra HSR Trains to Jiuzhaigou, Everest and Namtso Reopen & Altay Lifetime Free Passes**
*Compiled and reviewed by YouTu Travel*
*Based on official announcements and transport authority notices.*

Good evening. Today is November 2, 2026. Welcome to the YouTu China Travel Daily Digest. Here are today's major field mobility and operational transit updates across the country.

In Tibet, major high-altitude scenic areas reopened following emergency snow clearance. The Mount Everest Scenic Area in Shigatse resumed visitor entry at 14:00 after mountain roads were cleared, though Base Camp sectors remain icy; Lake Namtso also resumed operations and ticket sales as the Nagenla Mountain Pass cleared, with full refunds processed for affected reservations.
*Sources: [Tibet Department of Culture & Tourism, Shigatse Public Security Bureau](https://weibo.com/u/2061847537) · Everest & Lake Namtso alpine snow clearance & scenic reopening notice*

To manage peak autumn foliage crowds, China Railway Chengdu Group deployed additional passenger trains along the Sichuan-Qinghai Railway connecting Chengdu East directly to [Huanglong Jiuzhai](/west), Mount Emei, and Songpan through November 29. Railway authorities noted that tickets from Chengdu toward Xi'an and Beijing around November 11 are already selling out fast.
*Source: [China Railway Chengdu Group](https://weibo.com/u/1919955065) · Sichuan-Qinghai Railway extra autumn foliage bullet trains timetable*

In Xinjiang, Altay's Habahe County introduced a single-ticket lifetime free admission policy covering Baihaba Village and Birch Forest, while Tianshan Tianchi shifted to its 45 RMB winter rate with admission waivers for guests of partner hotels. In the south, tickets opened on 12306 for the dedicated Y878/7 'Culinary Tourism Express' linking Nanning, Liuzhou, and [Guilin](/south).
*Sources: [Xinjiang Department of Culture & Tourism, China Railway Nanning Group](https://weibo.com/u/2479930984) · Altay lifetime pass policy, Tianchi winter rates & Nanning tourist train ticketing*

Motorists should note that due to lane expansion works on the G4 Expressway between Yueyang and Changsha, Hunan's Dajing and Bancang toll plazas will close from November 4 through January 20, 2027, requiring detours via Highway 107.
*Source: [Hunan Department of Transportation](https://weibo.com/u/2164623652) · G4 Expressway lane reconstruction & toll plaza diversion directive*

That concludes today's China travel bulletin. See you tomorrow.
