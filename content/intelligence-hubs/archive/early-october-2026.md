### October 14, 2026 &bull; 21:14 CST
#### Yala Snow Mountain Opens Autumn-Winter Self-Drive Entry with Daily Vehicle Quota

> **🇨🇳 官方通告原文 (Source Dispatch)**:  
> 近日，亚拉雪山景区发布通知，每年10月中旬至次年4月底，景区开放私家车自驾入园。 自驾入园仅限7座及以下非营运私家车，营运车辆、摩托车不可入园。每日入园车辆限额20台，订满即止，需提前1天预约。入园时段为预约当日10:30-16:30，当日18:00前须驶出景区。5座车自驾票60元/车，7座车自驾票90元/车。 自驾入园后，车辆全程限速30km/h，游客需遵守景区管理规定，保护高原生态，禁止驶入未开放区域。 via：牧师、

**🇬🇧 Actionable Travel Intel**:  
The Yala Snow Mountain scenic area has announced that from mid-October to the end of April each year, the scenic area opens self-drive entry for private cars. Only non-commercial private cars with 7 seats or fewer are permitted; commercial vehicles and motorcycles may not enter. The daily quota is 20 vehicles on a first-come basis, and bookings must be made at least 1 day in advance. The entry window is 10:30 to 16:30 on the booked day, and vehicles must leave the scenic area before 18:00. The self-drive ticket costs 60 yuan for a 5-seat car and 90 yuan for a 7-seat car. Once inside, vehicles must observe the scenic area speed limit, and visitors must follow management rules, protect the high-altitude ecosystem and stay out of closed areas.

> **Verified Source**: [Garze Prefecture Bureau of Culture and Tourism (甘孜文旅, Sina Weibo)](https://weibo.com/2014827750/RmFz51PPH)

**🇬🇧 Actionable Travel Intel**:  
China's national meteorological authority forecasts a new cold wave moving from west to east across most of China from October 15 to 19. Northern Xinjiang, Inner Mongolia, North China, the Northeast, Huanghuai, Jianghuai, Jianghan and western and northern Jiangnan will see temperature drops of 4 to 6 degrees Celsius, with local drops above 8 degrees, accompanied in some areas by level 4 to 5 northerly winds and gusts of level 6 to 8. Moderate to heavy rain, locally torrential, is forecast for eastern Northwest China, North China, the Northeast, Huanghuai, Jianghuai, Jianghan, northern Jiangnan and Chongqing, while rain and snow are expected in high-altitude areas of northern Xinjiang, Gansu and Qinghai and in northeastern Inner Mongolia and northern Heilongjiang. From the night of October 14 to the daytime of October 15, heavy to torrential rain is forecast in parts of southeastern Chongqing, northern Guizhou, southern Hubei, northern Hunan, northern Jiangxi and central Liaoning, with locally severe torrential rain in northern Hunan and southeastern Hubei. Short-duration intense rainfall, thunderstorm gales and other severe convective weather are also possible in these areas.

> **🇨🇳 官方通告原文 (Source Dispatch)**:

**🇬🇧 Actionable Travel Intel**:  
The Gansu Provincial Meteorological Bureau announced a major weather notice: affected by cold air and a plateau trough, the province will experience a large-scale rain, snow, and temperature-drop event from October 15 to 17. Most of the Hedong region will see light to moderate rain, with heavy rain in eastern Longzhong and eastern Longdong, and maximum cumulative precipitation of 30-50 mm. The Gannan Plateau, Taizi Mountain area, and high-altitude areas of Hexi will see sleet turning to light to moderate snow, with heavy snow in the Qilian Mountains, maximum cumulative precipitation of 8-15 mm, and maximum snow depth of 4-6 cm. From October 16 to 17, temperatures across most of the province will drop markedly from west to east, with the minimum temperature in western Hexi falling by 6-8 degrees Celsius. Tourists are advised to monitor weather changes closely, check destination and en-route weather and scenic-area opening status before traveling, and to postpone or adjust itineraries if extreme weather warnings are issued, avoiding areas prone to disasters. The advisory also asks scenic areas to implement rain, snow, and temperature-drop precautions.
