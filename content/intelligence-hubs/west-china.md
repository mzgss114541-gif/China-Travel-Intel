**🇬🇧 Actionable Travel Intel**:  
The Gansu Provincial Meteorological Bureau announced a major weather notice: affected by cold air and a plateau trough, the province will experience a large-scale rain, snow, and temperature-drop event from October 15 to 17. Most of the Hedong region will see light to moderate rain, with heavy rain in eastern Longzhong and eastern Longdong, and maximum cumulative precipitation of 30-50 mm. The Gannan Plateau, Taizi Mountain area, and high-altitude areas of Hexi will see sleet turning to light to moderate snow, with heavy snow in the Qilian Mountains, maximum cumulative precipitation of 8-15 mm, and maximum snow depth of 4-6 cm. From October 16 to 17, temperatures across most of the province will drop markedly from west to east, with the minimum temperature in western Hexi falling by 6-8 degrees Celsius. Tourists are advised to monitor weather changes closely, check destination and en-route weather and scenic-area opening status before traveling, and to postpone or adjust itineraries if extreme weather warnings are issued, avoiding areas prone to disasters. The advisory also asks scenic areas to implement rain, snow, and temperature-drop precautions.

### September 1, 2026 &bull; 18:33 CST
#### Daocheng Yading Enforces Strict Ban on Unauthorized Trekking in Core Reserve

> **🇨🇳 官方通告原文 (Source Dispatch)**:  
> 分类： 景区公告 日期：2026年9月01日 广大游客、旅行社、户外运动组织及社会各界： 稻城亚丁是世界人自然与生物圈保护区、国家级自然保护区、省级风景名胜区，高原生态系统极其脆弱，未开放区域海拔高、地形复杂、气候多变，氧气稀薄、昼夜温差极大，存在雪崩、落石、沼泽、大雾迷路、高原急性疾病等极高生命风险，无通信信号、无应急救援保障条件。为严格保护高原生态环境，防范人员伤亡事故，依据《中华人民共和国生态环境法典》《中华人民共和国青藏高原生态保护法》《中华人民共和国旅游法》《中华人民共和国自然保护区条例》《风景名胜区条例》《四川省风景名胜区条例》等法律法规，现就严禁非法穿越有关事项公告如下： 一、严格管控活动区域。 亚丁国家级自然保护区核心保护区，除法定科研监测、管护巡护、应急救援等情形外，禁止一切人为活动；一般控制区未经主管部门审批，严禁开展穿越、徒步、探险、露营等活动。

**🇬🇧 Actionable Travel Intel**:  
On September 1, 2026, Daocheng Yading issued an official notice strictly prohibiting illegal crossing and unauthorized outdoor activities to protect its fragile plateau ecosystem and prevent casualties, noting that unopened areas feature high altitude, complex terrain, volatile weather, thin oxygen, extreme day-night temperature swings, hazards including avalanches, rockfalls, marshes, dense fog, and acute altitude sickness, and no communication signals or emergency rescue conditions. Within the core protection zone of Yading National Nature Reserve, all human activities are prohibited except statutory scientific monitoring, conservation patrols, and emergency rescue; within the general control zone, crossing, hiking, exploration, and camping without competent authority approval are strictly forbidden.
