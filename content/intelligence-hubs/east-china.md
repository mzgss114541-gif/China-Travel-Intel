  <li><strong style="color: #2c2825;">Mount Huangshan Cable Cars</strong>: Yungu and Yuping cableways manage queues via digital dispatching. Boarding queues can exceed 90 minutes after 10:00 on clear autumn mornings; an early 06:30 departure is essential.</li>
