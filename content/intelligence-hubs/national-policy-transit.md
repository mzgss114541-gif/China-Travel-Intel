<h3 style="font-family: Georgia, serif; font-size: 19px; color: #2c2825; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 25px; margin-bottom: 10px;">Understanding the "Third Country/Territory" Requirement for 144h Transit</h3>
<p style="color: #524b45; font-size: 15px; line-height: 1.75;">If you are traveling under the 144-hour transit scheme, your departure ticket from China <strong style="color: #2c2825;">cannot return to your port of embarkation</strong>. A flight routing of <em>London &rarr; Shanghai &rarr; London</em> is not eligible. However, a routing of <em>London &rarr; Shanghai &rarr; Hong Kong &rarr; London</em> is 100% compliant, as Hong Kong, Macau, and Taiwan are legally recognized as third transit regions.</p>
